Questions

Good to know.

How many bottles does it hold?

72 standard 750 ml bottles in individual slots, plus an angled display shelf along the top for your favourite labels.

Does it come assembled?

It ships flat-packed with all hardware and takes about 30–45 minutes with a screwdriver. Assembly is available in Melbourne metro — ask us.

How much is delivery?

Free to metro areas on the Single, and free Australia-wide on the Pair. Regional delivery for a Single is quoted at checkout. VIC orders usually arrive in 2–4 business days, other states in 4–8.

Is it safe with kids around?

Every rack includes a wall-anchoring kit. Like any tall free-standing furniture, please anchor it to the wall.

Can I stack or join them?

Yes — set them side by side to build a continuous wall. The Pair includes joining hardware.

What's your returns policy?

30 days for change of mind (unused, in original packaging). Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law always apply. See shipping & returns.
