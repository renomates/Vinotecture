Shipping & returns

Delivery

  • Dispatched from Melbourne within 1–2 business days.

Returns

Change your mind within 30 days of delivery and we'll refund you, as long as the rack is unused and in its original packaging. Return shipping for change-of-mind returns is at your cost.

Faulty or damaged

If your rack arrives damaged or faulty, email photos to hello@vinotecture.com.au within 7 days and we'll replace or refund it. Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law.
