9 wine cellar ideas for small spaces

You don't need a basement for a cellar. These ideas work in Australian townhouses, apartments and new builds.

1. Under-stair wine nook

The classic. Dark, internal and usually wasted space. A 1 m² rack fits under most staircases.

2. Butler's pantry wall

Builders are adding butler's pantries to almost every new home — one wall of wine racking turns it into a feature.

3. Dining room feature wall

Put the collection where you drink it. A rack with a display top reads as furniture, not storage.

4. Hallway gallery

A 30 cm-deep rack fits most hallways without blocking traffic.

5. Converted coat cupboard

Remove the hanging rail and drop a rack in. Add a door for darkness.

6. Home bar backdrop

Pair a rack with open shelving for glasses and spirits.

7. Display-home showpiece

For builders: a wine wall is a low-cost, high-impact styling moment for walkthroughs.

8. Two-module corner

Two racks at right angles make a corner cellar with 144 bottles.

9. Full cellar wall

Repeat 1 m modules across a wall for a custom look without custom joinery.
