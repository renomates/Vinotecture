How to store wine at home in Australia (without a cellar)

Most Australian homes don't have a basement, and our summers are hard on wine. The good news: you don't need a cellar to store wine well for a few years. You need four things — stable temperature, darkness, the right bottle position and a little humidity.

1. Keep the temperature steady

Wine ages best at roughly 12–16°C, but stability matters more than the exact number. Big daily swings push wine in and out past the cork. An internal wall away from western sun and the kitchen beats a garage every time.

2. Keep it dark

UV light degrades wine, especially in clear and green bottles. Avoid windows and skylights; a hallway, under-stair space or interior pantry is ideal.

3. Store bottles on their side

Lying cork-sealed bottles horizontally keeps the cork moist so it doesn't shrink and let air in. Screw caps (most Aussie wine) don't need it, but horizontal racks are still the most space-efficient way to store them.

4. Mind the humidity

Around 50–70% is comfortable. Melbourne and Sydney homes are usually fine; in very dry inland or air-conditioned homes, keep racks away from heating and cooling vents.

Which room is best?

RoomVerdict
Under the stairsGreat — dark, internal, stable
Butler's pantryGreat — and handy
Hallway / dining wallGood, if out of direct sun
KitchenAvoid — heat from cooking and appliances
GarageAvoid — summer heat swings

How much storage do you need?

A simple rule: buy storage for twice what you own today. If you buy a case a month, a 72-bottle rack fills in about six months.
