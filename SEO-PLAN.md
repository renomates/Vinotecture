# Vinotecture — SEO & 50-Sale Launch Plan

**Goal:** sell 50 Gallery 100 racks in ~8 weeks (pre-Christmas → late summer).
**Reality check:** a new domain rarely ranks on Google in its first 2–3 months. SEO is the long game; the first 50 sales will mostly come from paid search, Marketplace, social and trade outreach. Both run in parallel from day one.

---

Ownership rule: one main keyword per page, no two pages chasing the same term.

## 3. On-page (already built in)

- Unique `<title>` / meta description per page, `en-AU`, canonical tags
- Product + Organization + FAQ JSON-LD (rich results for price, stock, FAQ)
- Article JSON-LD on journal posts
- Open Graph image for social shares
- `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, custom 404
- Static site = very fast Core Web Vitals; keep images WebP < 200 KB with `width`/`height`
- Descriptive alt text; internal links from every article to the product

**To do once live:** real product photos with keyword file names (`100-bottle-timber-wine-rack-display.webp`); add `aggregateRating` to the schema only once you have genuine reviews.

## 5. Local & off-page

- **Google Business Profile** (Melbourne, service-area business) — photos, product listing, ask every buyer for a review.
- **Google Merchant Center** — free product listings in Google Shopping; the JSON-LD already supplies the data.
- **Marketplace listings that link back:** Bunnings Marketplace, Amazon AU, eBay, Temple & Webster supplier programme.
- **Links/PR:** pitch "a 100-bottle wine wall for under A$650" to The Interiors Addict, Houzz AU, Domain/realestate.com.au lifestyle sections, local wine bloggers. Send a free rack to 2–3 interior stylists in exchange for photos.
- **Directories:** Houzz pro profile, Hipages, True Local, Yelp AU.

## 6. The fast 50 (what actually sells the first units)

| Channel | Action | Budget | Target sales |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Ads (Search + Shopping) | Exact/phrase on money keywords, AU only; Shopping via Merchant Center | A$40/day | 15 |
| Facebook Marketplace | List in Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane; styled photo; "delivered" | Free | 10 |
| Meta/Instagram ads | 15-sec reel: empty wall → wine wall. Audiences: wine interest, new home owners, renovators | A$25/day | 8 |
| Builder outreach | List 100 VIC builders / display homes / cabinet makers; email + visit with spec sheet; 1 trade deal = 3–10 units | Time | 12 |
| Wine clubs & bottle shops | Flyer + 10% code for members; offer a display rack to 3 bottle shops | ~A$300 | 5 |

**Target cost per sale:** under A$80. Stop any channel above A$120 after 10 days.

**Offer mechanics:** Founding-50 price, the Pair bundle, A$50 back for an install photo, Christmas delivery cut-off banner (VIC ~Dec 15, others ~Dec 10).

---

## 7. Measure weekly

- Sessions, conversion rate (aim 1.5–3%), cost per sale by channel (GA4 `begin_checkout` + Stripe sales)
- Search Console: impressions/clicks for money keywords, indexed pages
- Trade leads and quote-to-order rate

## 8. 90-day SEO milestones

- **Day 7:** indexed in Google, Merchant Center live, GBP verified
- **Day 30:** 6 articles, 5 genuine reviews, 3 backlinks
- **Day 60:** page 2–3 for "wine rack cabinet", ranking for long-tail article terms
- **Day 90:** 10+ articles, 15 reviews, organic traffic covering 20%+ of sales
